# Community Code of Conduct

Prismtek aims to be a useful, welcoming place for players, addon authors, artists, and contributors.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and discuss the work rather than attacking people.
- Give credit and respect authors' licenses, attribution, and distribution choices.
- Offer actionable feedback and reproduce bugs where possible.
- Respect privacy; do not share another person's private data or conversations.
- No harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, spam, or attempts to impersonate creators.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, and restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and useful.

Report community concerns privately to **prismtek@prismtek.dev** when a public issue is inappropriate. Do not post sensitive personal information in public reports.

This policy applies to the repository's project discussions, issues, and contributions.
